Field guide

Monochroma was drawn in color once. The First Ember burned it down to four shades, and the Snakes are what crawled out of the ash. They grow by eating bits — the crumbs of the old world — and they change by eating relics. The color was never destroyed: it is locked inside the gray blocks. Every block you break with a spat relic lets some out. Five steps bring it back — green (grass, leaves), blue (water, sky), earth (stone, wood, walls) and finally life, when every snake finds its color. A fallen tower counts triple. Beyond the edge is the unrendered void. Don't fall off.

Steer

  • 1Hold both hands up like a wheel. Lower the left hand to curve left, the right hand to curve right. No camera: ← →.
  • 2Open your mouth to spit the NEXT relic (or press F). Keys 1–5 spit a specific card. Space pauses. Every spit also smashes what it hits — trees, stone, walls, even the Keep's towers (3 hits).
  • 3Hitting a head = K.O. Hitting a body severs the tail — and any relics growing on it fall off for anyone to grab.
